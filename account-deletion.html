Eco Clicker account deletion

This page applies to Eco Clicker: Ocean Cleanup by Axion Lab.

Delete inside the app

  1. Open Eco Clicker.
  2. Tap Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account & local profile and confirm.

If Google requires a recent login, sign in with Google again and repeat the deletion step.

Request deletion by email

Email playmkzp@gmail.com with the subject “Eco Clicker account deletion”. Include the captain name or Google email used for the account so the request can be located.

What is deleted

The Firebase Authentication account, its user identifier, display name and associated authentication details are deleted. Locally saved captain-profile and analytics-consent settings are removed by the in-app deletion action.

What is retained

Game progress remains stored locally unless you clear app storage or uninstall the game. Google Play purchase records are managed by Google and may be retained for transaction, fraud-prevention, tax and legal obligations. Deletion requests are normally completed within 30 days.
