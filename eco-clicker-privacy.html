Eco Clicker: Ocean Cleanup — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

Core gameplay works offline. Optional account, analytics, advertising and purchase services use Google Firebase, Google AdMob and Google Play. Axion Lab does not sell personal data.

Information processed

When you choose guest or Google sign-in, Firebase Authentication may process an account identifier, display name, email address, IP address and device or user-agent information. If you explicitly allow optional gameplay analytics, Google Analytics for Firebase may process app interactions, app-instance identifiers, device information, approximate location derived from network information, app version and diagnostics. Analytics is disabled until you opt in, and advertising-personalization signals are disabled by the game.

Advertising and purchases

Google AdMob serves banner and rewarded ads and may process device or account identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, app interactions and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, subject to applicable consent choices. Google Play Billing processes Eco Premium purchases. Payment details are collected directly by Google Play and are not accessible to Axion Lab.

Local game data

Game progress, settings, advertising-reward cooldowns and premium-use counters are stored locally. Clear app storage or uninstall to remove them. Google Play separately records purchases so eligible purchases can be restored.

Your choices

You can decline gameplay analytics and still play, or change the choice from Profile. Applicable advertising consent is requested through Google's consent form. To remove an account, follow the account deletion instructions.

Security and service providers

Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit. Service-dashboard access is restricted to the developer account. See the Google Privacy Policy, Firebase privacy information and AdMob privacy information.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact playmkzp@gmail.com.
